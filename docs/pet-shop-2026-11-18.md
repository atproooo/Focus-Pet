# Pet, shop and hatching implementation — 2026-11-18

- Hatching requires one Pet Egg and 100 Essence. The hatch button is disabled with gray text and background when either resource is missing. Interrupted requests offer recovery instead of a second charge.
- A successful hatch opens a dedicated result screen with the pet's name, species, rarity and level. Users can choose it as their companion, open its details or return to the collection. The result survives reopening until the user navigates away.
- The focus screen includes a reusable animation container bound to the selected companion and its image key. No artwork, animation assets or visual effects were added.
- Pet details provide Pet, Play and Rest interactions. They persist mood/energy changes, clamp values to 0–100 and share a ten-second per-pet cooldown. Interactions grant no XP, focus progress or currency.
- The collection and resource chips lead to the shop. Coins buy permanent cosmetic unlocks; Essence is spent on hatching. Pet Eggs are not sold in the shop — they drop from focus sessions over time. Owned cosmetics can be equipped or removed per pet through Customize. Slot/category keys remain extensible, and the existing standalone product/cosmetic data types are now compiled into the app.
- Initial catalog prices are centralized in ShopCatalog.java: the collar, shirt and decoration cost 25, 50 and 75 Coins respectively. Pet Eggs are not sold in the shop; they are earned through focus drops, and hatching an egg costs 100 Essence. These are functional starting values for later balancing.
- Purchase deductions, grants and idempotent receipts are persisted together in one SharedPreferences edit under a shared lock. Existing currency storage is retained. Hatching uses a durable preference journal and a unique Room pet request key to recover before/after an interrupted insert without creating another pet or charging again.
- Room migrates from v4 to v5 without deleting existing pets or focus history. Added fields track hatch requests and the last interaction time.
- Removed the extra four-minute early-end warning. The normal single end-session confirmation and discounted reward/progress rules remain.

Validation: debug/release builds, ten local tests and 37 distinct API 37 device cases passed. Tests include disabled hatch states, result restoration, shop/wardrobe UI, cancellation without charge, concurrent purchase safety, duplicate requests, interaction limits, recovery journals, v4-to-v5 migration, an early end after five simulated focused minutes, and existing timer/history/goal flows including a real one-minute background session. The isolated test application uses a separate package. Real-device, long-term power-management and storage-failure testing remain outside this pass.
